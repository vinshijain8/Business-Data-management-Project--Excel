# Business-Data-management-Project--Excel
“NAVKAR ENTERPRISES” is a manufacturing company specializing in stickers, including  customized, logo, and cartoon varieties. The organization faced significant challenges related to  inventory inefficiencies, high material wastage—particularly of costly inputs like adhesive and  silicone—and pricing pressures in a competitive market. 

To address these challenges, comprehensive data was collected encompassing inventory usage, 
sales performance, wastage records, and competitor pricing. Descriptive statistics and analyses 
were conducted using tools such as Excel and Python. Key methodologies included Pareto 
Analysis to prioritize high-revenue sticker categories, What-If Analysis to simulate production 
and material usage scenarios, and trend analysis to identify demand patterns and variability 
across product types. These approaches provided a quantitative foundation for understanding 
turnover ratios, stock management gaps, and sales dynamics. 

The analysis revealed that approximately 20% of sticker types contributed to 80% of revenue, 
directing focus toward optimizing these segments. Material wastage was substantial and variable, 
especially for adhesive and silicone, indicating inefficiencies in handling and procurement. 
Demand for top-selling stickers remained stable, while cartoon stickers exhibited greater 
volatility. These findings informed smarter procurement and inventory strategies aimed at 
reducing overstocking and stockouts, with an emphasis on perishable and high-cost materials. 

As a result of implementing data-driven recommendations, NAVKAR ENTERPRISES observed 
improved inventory control, reduced material waste, and more aligned purchasing practices. This 
led to cost savings, enhanced operational efficiency, and better positioning against competitive 
pricing pressures, demonstrating the value of leveraging analytics for sustainable manufacturing 
growth.
